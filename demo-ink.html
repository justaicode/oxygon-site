Fuelling

A plan for every checkpoint

Set hourly targets for water, carbohydrate and sodium — or let Oxygon suggest them from your sweat rate, gut ceiling and the forecast. It spreads the doses across your checkpoints; hand-edit any stop and recalculation never overwrites it.

Coach

A training block that adapts

A guided interview builds your dated plan — Norwegian threshold or pace-based. Fitness, fatigue, form and durability tracked from Apple Health and Strava. Ask the coach anything; where it proposes a change, you confirm with a button — never applied silently.

Live

Built to be read mid-effort

Heart rate, glucose, GPS and conditions on one screen, your position on the elevation profile, and the next checkpoint with what to take. Every readout sized for a two-second glance — eighteen hours in, breathing hard.
