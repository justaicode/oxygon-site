The short version

Oxygon is a race-fuelling and training app for runners. Recording your runs, building a fuelling plan and using the calculators are free and need no account. Oxygon Pro is an optional subscription that adds crew live-tracking, more than one plan at a time, the AI coach and the progress charts. It renews by itself until you cancel, and you cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple ID settings — not in the app. Oxygon is not a medical service.

Oxygon Pro — the subscription

Oxygon Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. It includes:

  • Crew live-tracking — share a live link with your position, pace, heart rate, glucose and drift
  • Unlimited plans — keep every race you're building in flight at once
  • Ask your coach — an AI coach that has read your training and answers questions about it
  • Progress & insights — fitness, fatigue, form and durability across your whole history

It is sold in two lengths, monthly and annual. The exact price is shown in your own currency on the subscription screen before you confirm, because the stores set it per country — that screen, not this page, is the price you agree to.

Renewal. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period at the then-current price, and your account is charged within 24 hours of the end of the current period, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Cancelling. You cancel where you bought it — on Android in Google Play → Subscriptions, on iPhone and iPad in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. We cannot cancel it for you. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, and neither does deleting your Oxygon account. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the period you have paid for runs out.

Live tracking and your crew

Crew live-tracking publishes your position and readings to a private link for as long as an activity is running. Anyone who has that link can see it — sharing it is your choice, and so is who you send it to. We don't check who opens it.

Community

Community is optional and needs a sign-in. You share an activity on purpose. People who follow you can read that post, its photos, its caption and its comments. Any signed-in athlete can follow any other. Nothing is shared until you share it.

By posting you accept this section. The app asks for that acceptance before your first post.

Rules of conduct

  • Be decent to other athletes. No harassment, no threats, and no attacks on a person for who they are.
  • Post only what you have the right to post. Photos and video must be yours, or you must have permission to share them.
  • Don't post another person's private information, and don't post someone else's training as if it were yours.
  • Don't post anything illegal, including sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Community is for sharing your own activities with followers. Don't use it to advertise, or to collect other athletes' data.

The rest of these terms still apply, including the rule that Oxygon is not a medical service. A heart rate or a glucose figure on a post is a number from your activity, not advice.

What gets removed

  • We remove posts, comments, photos and profile pictures that break these rules.
  • You can unshare your own post. That removes the post and its photos.
  • You can delete your own comment. The author of a post can delete a comment on that post.

Blocking

You can block another athlete. Blocking hides each of you from the other: you do not see their posts, and they do not see yours. Either person can block.

We read reports and remove what breaks these rules. A reported post stays up until we have reviewed it, except that it is hidden from the person who reported it.
