1.3.11 — 30 October 2026

On Google Play. Not an App Store version. The App Store app is still 1.3.10.

  • A finished recording uploads to Strava on its own, even if you close the app, and retries until it succeeds.
  • Heart rate keeps recording when a strap drops out.
  • Add photos and video to any finished activity, Sauna included.
  • That week shows Run pace, with Climb in metres beside the week before.
  • Sauna, yoga and strength are their own sessions. A sauna fills toward the time you set.
  • The month shows each sport, planned time in grey.
  • A run with no distance says Not recorded.
  • A live run stays open.

Widgets, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island stay on iPhone.

This week — 29 October 2026

On the web app now.

  • That week shows your average run pace, or your ride speed, and how it compares with the week before. The current week includes the sessions you have planned.
  • Someone who opens your live-tracking link after you have started sees the route so far, not only where you are now.
  • On the web app, a wellness day can show weight, resting heart rate, sleep and heart-rate variability. A run with no distance says Not recorded. Tiles on a live-tracking page can be swapped, and cardiac drift draws a trend line.

Not on the App Store yet

Not in the App Store version on sale today. The web app already shows the route so far on a live link.

  • On iPhone, someone who opens your live-tracking link after you have started sees the route so far. The web app already does this.
  • The summary opens as soon as you finish.
  • A share to Strava includes the map. If you delete it on Strava, you can share it again.
  • Yoga, sauna and strength can be shared, and a saved share card keeps a solid background.
  • You can open an activity from its Community post and edit it.
  • Titles such as average pace stay on one line.

1.3.10 — 28 October 2026

On the App Store. This is the update after 1.3.3. The same weeks are on the web app.

  • Share a 3D video of a run or a ride. The camera follows the route through the turns. Or share a sticker: your stats, the route, the splits, the day, a big number, or a map.
  • Race Predictor sets every distance from 5K to the marathon, plus 60K, 80K and 100K, from your best recent run. One line names that run.
  • Plan a run, ride, swim, strength session, yoga or sauna on one page, and open it from the calendar ready to start. A fuel block puts the doses on the plan and on the live screen.
  • The calendar can hold a note for a day or several days: limited availability, no availability, illness, injury, recovery, or a plain note. A missed plan keeps its color and gains a ring once the day is over.
  • When the same outing is recorded more than once, the Garmin figures are the ones that count.
  • Connect a heart-rate strap or watch, a treadmill or a foot pod, a Wahoo KICKR, and power and cadence pedals. A saved heart-rate source connects on its own.
  • During a sauna you can change the temperature. A finished sauna or yoga shows its heart-rate chart.
  • Post to Community or share to Strava when you finish. You choose what goes out, including hiding the ends of the route. Read and accept the Community rules before the first post.
  • A ride replay lasts at least as long as you were moving.
  • On iPhone, Home Screen widgets follow the session. The Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island show distance, pace, time and heart rate during a session.

On Google Play the public app is 1.3.11, from 30 October 2026. What that update adds is in the 1.3.11 entry. Widgets, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island are iPhone only.

1.3.3 — 1 October 2026

On the App Store. It replaced 1.3.1.

  • Plan what you will eat and drink, checkpoint by checkpoint, and draw the course you will do it on.
  • Record with GPS, heart rate and auto-pause, and share a link so your crew can follow your position, pace and heart rate.
  • Afterward, see the pace, the heart rate against the course, the route, and the shoes you used.
  • Eight calculators work with no account. A library of real races is there to plan against.
  • Community, an optional account, and sync between your phone and the web app are in this version.

August 2026

The App Store app was public by 10 August 2026. Version 1.2 was on sale on 24 August 2026.

  • Pace, race prediction, grade-adjusted pace, heart-rate zones, training paces and calories, on the phone, with no account.
  • Race fuelling: hourly water, carbohydrate and sodium, spread across the checkpoints.
  • From 24 August: Garmin, Coros, Polar, Suunto and Wahoo can bring workouts in, with heart rate and the route. Shoes count the kilometres on each pair.
